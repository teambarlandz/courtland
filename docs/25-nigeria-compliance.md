# 25 — Nigeria compliance

Courtland operates in Nigeria and handles residential leases, land, and money on behalf of private
landlords. This document records the obligations that shape the product, and where the product makes an
assumption that a lawyer must confirm.

**This is engineering guidance, not legal advice.** Every section marked **[confirm]** needs a Nigerian
property lawyer's sign-off before launch. The lawyer engaged for this work is listed in the launch
checklist.

## 1. Jurisdiction

| Instrument | What it covers | Relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Rent Control Act 2023 | Residential tenancies | Rent increase cap, notice, records, landlord's duties |
| National Housing Act 2014 | General housing law | Framework |
| NUGA 1990 | Urban and Rural Development | Development control, building permits |
| Personal Income Tax Act | Landlord's tax | Courtland's role in reporting |
| Companies Act 2020 | Business obligations | Statutory records |
| Money Laundering Act / NFIU | AML/CFT | KYC and record-keeping |
| Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) | Processing of personal data | Lawful basis, rights, security |
| Consumer Protection Act | Fair dealing | Contract terms, advertising |
| Cybercrimes Act | Cybercrime | Incident obligations |
| Transfer of Property Act | Land transactions | Registration, title |
| State land laws | Lagos, FCT, Rivers, Ogun, Kano, and others | Notice periods, rents, rates |

State laws differ materially. Lagos State under the Lagos State Tenancy Law 2015 and the Rent Control Act
has provisions the other states do not. v1 supports Lagos and the FCT properly and records the jurisdiction
on every contract, so other states can be added with template and period changes rather than a code change.

## 2. Rent Control Act 2023

### 2.1 The cap

| Tenure | Annual increase |
|---|---|
| First five years of the tenancy | Not more than 10% |
| After five years | Not more than 10%, subject to the state's published guideline |
| A tenant in occupation for 10 years or more | Protected in occupation, on the terms in the Rent Control Act |

**What Courtland enforces.** The API refuses a proposed escalation above 10% of the current rent, with a
422 explaining the cap. A lease at ₦4,500,000 can renew at up to ₦4,950,000, and the system will not
produce a schedule at ₦5,400,000.

The 10% is computed on the contractual rent, not on a market index, and is checked at contract creation,
at renewal, and whenever staff edit a rent figure.

### 2.2 The notice period

| Situation | Notice |
|---|---|
| A tenant on a fixed term of more than one year, where the landlord intends not to renew | 6 months' written notice **[confirm]** |
| A tenant on a monthly tenancy | 1 month's written notice **[confirm]** |
| A tenant intending to vacate | As the contract states; 2 months is customary **[confirm]** |
| Eviction for cause | Court process. Courtland does not evict; it terminates a contract and issues a notice |

Notices are generated by the `notices` system, sent by the tenant's chosen channels, and stored with
delivery evidence. Under the Rent Control Act the landlord must keep records; Courtland is the landlord's
agent, so those records are the platform's records.

### 2.3 Records the landlord must keep

The Act requires landlords to keep certain records. Courtland stores:

| Record | Where | Retention |
|---|---|---|
| The signed tenancy agreement | `documents`, kind `agreement`, status `released` | 7 years after termination |
| Rent received, with dates | `payments_ledger` | 7 years |
| Payment receipts given to the tenant | `documents`, kind `receipt` | 7 years |
| Notices served, with delivery evidence | `notices` | 7 years |
| The tenant's identity | `profiles`, `contract_parties` | 7 years after the relationship ends |
| Maintenance requests and actions | `maintenance_tickets` | 7 years after the ticket closes |
| A statement of account | `documents`, kind `statement`, generated monthly | 7 years |

**[confirm]** Whether monthly statements satisfy the statutory record-keeping requirement, or whether a
particular form is mandated.

### 2.4 Landlord duties the platform records

| Duty | How Courtland records it |
|---|---|
| Give a receipt for rent received | Every payment generates a receipt the tenant can download |
| Maintain the property in a condition fit for habitation | `maintenance_tickets` with resolution dates, visible to the tenant |
| Not to evict a tenant without due process | Contracts terminate only through a documented process with notice. There is no "lock out" feature |
| Not to enter the premises without notice | The agreement template includes an access clause with notice |

## 3. Land and property transactions

| Requirement | Handling |
|---|---|
| A registered land title or its reference is required for a `sale` | `owners` holds the title reference and a `documents` row of kind `title`. `document_release` is blocked without it |
| Registry fees and stamp duties | **[confirm]** Whether Courtland should compute or collect these, or disclose them. v1 does neither; the agreement template names them as the buyer's or seller's cost |
| The seller's authority to sell | `owners` records whether the owner is the registered owner or an authorised agent, with a power of attorney as a document when it is an agent |
| Development control approval | For a building, `properties.land_details.building_approval_ref`. **[confirm]** whether a launch condition or a warning |
| Governor's consent | Required for a sale by a married couple in some states, and for certain land categories **[confirm]** |
| Survey and site plan | `land_details.boundary_geojson` and a site plan document. A plot without a boundary cannot be published |

Courtland's position is that it is an agent and a platform, not a conveyancer. The agreement PDF says so
explicitly, and the terms of service disclaim legal advice.

## 4. AML/CFT and KYC

**[confirm]** Whether Courtland is a covered institution requiring SECAML registration. If it is, the
obligations below become registration requirements rather than good practice.

### 4.1 What is collected

| Party | KYC | When |
|---|---|---|
| Owner, before the first payout | Government-issued ID, a selfie liveness check or a staff attestation, proof of address | Before `approved` |
| Owner, before a sale closes | The above plus the land title and a tax clearance certificate | Before `active` |
| Tenant | Government-issued ID, a selfie, a phone number, an address | Before `approved` |
| Guarantor | ID and address | Before `approved` |

### 4.2 Screening

| Requirement | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Sanctions screening | A list check against the UN, UK, US OFAC, and Nigerian lists, run at onboarding and daily for active parties **[confirm]** the required lists |
| Politically exposed persons | A flag on `owners.pep`, set during review, with senior approval required before proceeding |
| Adverse media | Not automated in v1. A manual review field on the KYC screen |
| Record of screening | `audit_log` on every KYC decision, with the reviewer and the timestamp |
| Refusal to proceed | A refusal is recorded with a reason. The customer is told the account cannot be opened, without saying why in detail |

Sanctions screening runs as a job and as a check in the approval path. If the screening service is
unavailable, approval is blocked rather than skipped. Failing open on a sanctions check is not acceptable.

### 4.3 Record keeping

| Record | Retention |
|---|---|
| KYC documents | 7 years after the relationship ends |
| KYC decisions, with the reviewer | 7 years |
| Screening results | 7 years |

## 5. Data protection: NDPA 2023

### 5.1 Data protection impact assessment

This is one. Handling identity documents, financial data, and location data about identifiable people is
exactly the kind of processing that requires one. It is a Phase 14 deliverable, owned jointly by the CTO
and the compliance lead.

### 5.2 Lawful basis

| Processing | Basis |
|---|---|
| Identity verification | Legal obligation, and contract performance |
| Contract and tenancy records | Contract performance |
| Payment and ledger records | Legal obligation |
| KYC records | Legal obligation |
| Notices and communications | Contract performance |
| Marketing email | Consent |
| Saved searches and alerts | Consent |
| Aggregate analytics | Legitimate interest |
| Security logs and the audit log | Legitimate interest, legal obligation |
| Retaining financial records after erasure | Legal obligation |

### 5.3 Data subject rights

| Right | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Right to be informed | The privacy policy, given at onboarding and linked from every communication |
| Right to access | `GET /v1/auth/me` plus a full export of the caller's data |
| Right to rectification | Name, phone, email, address. A change to an identity document goes through staff review |
| Right to erasure | Anonymise the profile. Financial and tenancy records are retained under the statutory obligation, and the privacy policy says so plainly |
| Right to data portability | A JSON or CSV export of the caller's data |
| Right to object | Marketing consent withdrawn. The transactional basis is unaffected |
| Right to withdraw consent | `profiles.marketing_consent_at = null` |
| Right to restrict processing | A `profiles.restricted_at` flag that stops marketing and non-essential processing while keeping the contract running |

**[confirm]** Whether the Data Protection Commission registration or notification applies to Courtland, and
whether a data protection officer must be appointed. **[confirm]** Whether the rights of a tenant who has
moved need to be exercisable by someone acting on their behalf.

### 5.4 Cross-border transfer

| Situation | Handling |
|---|---|
| Supabase, US or EU region | A transfer outside Nigeria **[confirm]** whether the NDPA requires a specific safeguard or notification, and whether an adequacy determination applies |
| Resend, Cloudinary, Inngest, Twilio | Processors outside Nigeria |
| Paystack | Nigerian |

If a safeguard is required, the mechanism is standard contractual clauses plus a transfer impact assessment
on each processor. Both are Phase 14 deliverables. In the meantime, processors are chosen with region
options where they exist, and the privacy policy names every processor.

### 5.5 Security obligations

The NDPA requires appropriate technical and organisational measures. The controls in
[`19-security.md`](./19-security.md) are the answer, and a breach of personal data must be reported to the
Data Protection Commission **[confirm]** the threshold and the timeline.

## 6. Consumer protection

| Obligation | Handling |
|---|---|
| No misleading advertising | A listing's photographs must be of the actual property. The publish checklist requires alt text and at least three photographs, and stock imagery is a listing-quality issue **[confirm]** whether there is a specific prohibition |
| Clear terms | Rent, period, deposit, service charge, and escalation cap are all explicit in the agreement and on the listing |
| No unfair terms | Clauses are composed from a validated set, not free text. A `custom_clauses` amendment is visually distinct and requires staff authority **[confirm]** whether any standard clause needs adjustment |
| Fair dispute resolution | A dispute process with defined stages, and the disputes are visible to both parties |
| Consumer complaints | A support channel with a defined response time, recorded |

## 7. Tax

**[confirm]** All of this with a Nigerian tax adviser.

| Question | Position |
|---|---|
| Is Courtland's management fee taxable? | Likely. The rate and the treatment need advice |
| Withholding on rent | Withholding tax on residential rent paid to a landlord is a real Nigerian obligation. Whether Courtland should withhold, and at what rate, needs advice. v1 does not withhold |
| VAT on the service charge | If the fee is above the registration threshold, VAT applies **[confirm]** |
| Is Courtland a withholding agent? | Only if the advice says so. If it is, the ledger needs a withholding allocation and the receipts need to show it |
| CGT on the sale of property | The buyer's or seller's obligation, not Courtland's. The agreement template names it |
| Stamp duty | On the contract. The template names it |

The v1 position is that Courtland passes money through and takes an agreed fee, and that tax compliance for
landlords is their own. If the advice says otherwise, the change is an allocation type and a receipt line,
not a rewrite.

## 9. Courtland's own position

Courtland is an agency and a platform. The terms of service say so, and every agreement template says so in
a prominent position.

| Courtland does | Courtland does not |
|---|---|
| Record agreements and payments | Give legal advice |
| Hold deposits and instalments in escrow | Hold a client's money as its own |
| Issue receipts and statements | Guarantee a landlord's title |
| Serve notices at a landlord's instruction | Enforce a tenancy |
| Screen for sanctions | Advise on a dispute |
| Provide a dispute process | Adjudicate a dispute |

A user's agreement template includes:

```
This Agreement is entered into between the parties named below and is administered by
Courtland Nigeria Ltd (RC 1234567), acting as the agents of the Landlord. Courtland is not a
party to this Agreement, is not a legal adviser to either party, and does not provide legal
services. Each party has had the opportunity to seek independent legal advice.
```

## 12. What this means for the build
